Alcove

Give every icon a home.

Alcove is a Windows desktop organizer. It replaces the loose grid of icons on your desktop with named groups — Alcoves — that open one at a time and keep their own arrangement per monitor.

What it does

  • Organizes your Desktop into drawers. On first run it sorts what is already there into Apps, Documents, Photos, Folders and Installers — or starts empty and lets you file things yourself.
  • Opens one drawer at a time. Click a tile in the rail to open it, click again to close. The wallpaper stays clear the rest of the time.
  • Grows into a canvas. Small drawers open as a compact panel. Past twelve items a drawer spreads across the desktop, where you can make named rows and drag icons between them.
  • Mirrors real folders. Point a drawer at a folder on disk and it shows that folder's live contents, with icon, list and details views.
  • Learns what you open. A frequent strip along the screen edge fills itself with what you actually use, and slots hold their position so nothing moves under your cursor.
  • Gives each monitor its own desk. Drawers belong to a screen, and the layout is saved per desk.
  • Keeps Windows working. Running apps stay on the taskbar, files open with their real shell associations, and the Recycle Bin behaves like the Recycle Bin.

Status

Alcove is in early development. Downloads land here when the first build is ready.
