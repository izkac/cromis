Give every icon a home. How we work

Fewer things, finished.

Three rules we have not found a good reason to break.

Native first

If the platform already does it, we use the platform. Real shell associations, the real Recycle Bin, the real taskbar. We organize what is there instead of replacing it with a lookalike.

Small surfaces

Fewer features, each of them finished. Nothing half-built shipped behind a flag, nothing kept because removing it would be awkward.

Your files stay yours

No account needed to use what you installed. No telemetry, no cloud round-trip, nothing copied or moved behind your back. Our software works with the network unplugged.

Buy the software, not a subscription to it.

Cromis software is complete without a licence key — every feature, every monitor, no time limit, no nag at startup. A licence buys updates for the period it covers. When it lapses, your copy keeps working forever, in full; you simply stop being offered newer builds. There is no activation server, nothing to revoke, and no check that can fail and lock you out.
