Alcove

Version 0.2.5 Windows desktop organizer

Give every icon a home. Alcove takes that loose grid of desktop icons and gives them named drawers instead, which open one at a time, spread out into a full canvas once they get crowded, and remember their own arrangement on every monitor you own. They pick up your wallpaper's colour while they're at it. Underneath, Alcove is reading your real Desktop folder and opening things with the real Windows shell, so nothing gets copied or moved anywhere unless you move it yourself.

02 How we work Three rules

We'd rather finish three things than start ten.

Three rules we keep coming back to, and haven't yet found a good reason to break.

Rule 01

Let Windows be Windows

If the platform already does the job, we let it. Real file associations. The real Recycle Bin. The real taskbar. We'd rather tidy up what's already on your machine than build a convincing copy of it and hope you never notice the seams.

Rule 02

Small surfaces

Fewer features, each one actually finished. We don't ship half-built things behind a feature flag, and we don't keep something around just because pulling it back out would be a hassle.

Rule 03

Your files stay yours

You shouldn't need an account to use software sitting on your own hard drive. So there's no sign-in, no telemetry, no quiet round-trip to a server, and nothing copied or moved behind your back. Unplug the network and everything still works.

03 Licence
